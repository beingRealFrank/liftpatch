CKEditor 4 to CKEditor 5 before Drupal 11

Drupal 10 ships with CKEditor 5 as its editor. Sites that carried CKEditor 4 text formats or custom CKEditor 4 plugins forward from Drupal 8 or 9 should clean that up while still on Drupal 10, so the Drupal 11 step is just a core move.

What to check

  • Text formats. At /admin/config/content/formats, open each format and confirm its editor is CKEditor 5, and that no buttons are missing or marked unsupported.
  • Contrib modules that add editor buttons. Some older plugins only worked with CKEditor 4. Check each one's project page for a CKEditor 5 or Drupal 11 release; the atlas lists the common ones.
  • Custom plugins. Anything in your custom modules that provides a CKEditor 4 plugin has to be rewritten for CKEditor 5.
  • Stored content. Check pages with embedded media, tables, inline styles and custom HTML after the switch. CKEditor 5 can strip markup that its configuration doesn't allow.

Plan

  1. On Drupal 10, switch each format to CKEditor 5 on a copy of the site and review the pages that use it.
  2. Replace or rewrite plugins that don't have a CKEditor 5 equivalent.
  3. Run Upgrade Status (commands here) and clear what it flags.
  4. Only then update core to 11.

Confirm details against the current Drupal documentation before you start; editor internals changed across 10.x releases.
