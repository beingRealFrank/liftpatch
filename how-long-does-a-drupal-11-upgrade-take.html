How long does a Drupal 10 to 11 upgrade take?

Plan on one to three working days of developer time for a small, well-maintained site, and one to several weeks for a site with abandoned modules, large custom code or no tests. Elapsed time is longer than effort because of review, staging and deploy windows. With Drupal 10 security support ending on December 9, 2026, start the measurement step now even if the work happens later.

Timeline by site type

These are planning ranges, not guarantees. The only reliable way to size your own site is to measure it.

Measure first (about an hour)

  1. Paste your composer.lock into the free scanner to see which modules block Drupal 11.
  2. Run Upgrade Status for custom-code findings (commands here).
  3. Count patches in composer.json; each one must still apply to the new version.

What stretches the calendar

  • Waiting for access or a staging site. Often the longest delay.
  • Client or stakeholder review. Allow time for editors to check content and forms.
  • Hosting. PHP 8.3 and Drush 13 must be available; see the requirements.
  • The deadline rush. Agencies and freelancers get busy as December 9 approaches.

Work back from December 9

For a site that goes live on Drupal 11 by December 9, measure now, then plan two releases: first the move to 10.6 with contrib updates, then core 11. Leave a week of buffer for review and rollback.
